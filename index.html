∿ Sine-wave generation

〰 Additive uniform noise

noisy[n] = original[n] + level × noise[n]
noise is in [−1, +1].

⚑ Digital filters

⌁ SNR calculation

SNR = 10 log₁₀(Σsignal² / Σerror²), in dB.
